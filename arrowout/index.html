Why Arrow Out

A logic puzzle that respects your time.

No timers, no energy bars, no friend-spam. Just clean puzzles that get smarter, one level at a time.

🧩

100 Hand-Crafted Levels

From gentle warm-ups to brain-melting 50×50 boards. Each puzzle is verified solvable by the same solver that powers your hints.

💡

Smart Hint System

Stuck? The bulb runs a full solvability search and points only at safe moves — never at a tap that fails or dead-ends the board.

🌙

📴

Plays Offline

🔇

Quiet by Design

Pinch-to-Zoom

How to Play

Three simple rules.

1

Tap an Arrow

Each arrow points one direction. Tap it and it slides that way until it leaves the board.

2

Don't Get Blocked

If another arrow is in the way, your move fails and you lose a life. Order matters.

3

Clear the Board

When every arrow has exited, the level is complete. Move on, or go back and try with fewer lives lost.
