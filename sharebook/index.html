One set of books.
Every partner.

Sharebook is shared bookkeeping for small businesses run by partners. Record the money, split profit automatically and settle up fairly, with payroll and team chat built in.

What it does

Built for partnerships.

Everyone sees the same numbers, so there are no arguments about who owes whom.

Shared ledger

Income, expenses, salaries, partner capital and withdrawals, and cash held by each partner, with a running balance.

Profit split & settlement

Automatic profit split by share, and a clear "who pays whom" settlement with a per-partner breakdown, per financial year.

Staff & payroll

Payroll runs with PDF payslips you can share on WhatsApp, plus attendance, leave and holidays.

Team chat

Direct and group chats with files, voice notes, GIFs, polls, daily stand-ups and @mentions.

More tools

Asset register, partner-to-partner loans, reports with Excel import and export, and notes.

Private by design

Privacy mode hides amounts, fingerprint app lock, roles and permissions, no ads, and usage analytics only if you opt in.
